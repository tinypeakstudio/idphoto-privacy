Privacy Policy – ID Photo Maker

Last updated: 25 October 2026

App: ID Photo Maker (Android), package com.tinypeakstudio.passportphoto

Developer: Tinypeak Studio

Contact: tinypeakstudio.app@gmail.com

ID Photo Maker helps you make passport, visa and ID photos and scanned signatures that match official size rules. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

In short:

  • Your photos and signatures are processed on your phone and are never uploaded.
  • There are no accounts: we never ask for your name, email or a password.
  • The app uses Google AdMob (ads), Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports) and Google Analytics for Firebase (anonymous usage statistics). None of them receive your photos.

1. Your photos and signatures stay on your phone

  • All photo processing happens on your device: background removal, face detection, cropping, quality checks, signature clean-up and compression.
  • Your photos and signatures are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. We do not run any server that receives them.
  • Face detection and background removal use Google ML Kit models that run on the device. The background-removal model may be downloaded once by Google Play services; this download contains no data about you or your photos.
  • While you work, images are kept only in the app's private storage on your phone. This storage is not part of your gallery and other apps cannot read it.
  • Your last photo (and last signature) is kept on the device for up to 24 hours so it can be restored if Android closes the app, for example while you share it. It is then deleted automatically. It is also deleted as soon as you start a new photo or signature.
  • Images leave the app's private storage only when you choose to save them to your gallery or share them with another app.
  • The app does not create or store face templates or biometric identifiers.

2. Permissions

Permission Why
Camera To take your ID photo or a photo of your signature. Used only while the camera screen is open.
Photos (system photo picker) To open a photo you pick. The app cannot browse your gallery.
Storage (Android 9 and older only) To save the finished file to your gallery. Newer Android versions need no permission for this.
Internet To show ads, to download the on-device background model, and to send crash reports and anonymous usage statistics. Never used for your photos or signatures.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner on the home screen, and a short rewarded video that unlocks saving, sharing and printing of each photo or signature.

  • AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form (UMP) at start-up, and no ad is requested before you have answered it. If you do not consent to personalised ads, only non-personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

Firebase Crashlytics (Google) collects crash and error information so we can fix bugs: device model, Android version, app version, a crash stack trace, and a random installation identifier. Crashes are reported automatically. Crash reports never include your photos or signatures. You can turn crash reports off in Settings → Send crash reports.

5. Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

Google Analytics for Firebase collects anonymous usage information so we can see how many people use the app and which features they use: app opens, how long the app is used, which screens of the app are opened (for example "Settings"), which document type a photo was made for (for example "US passport"), whether a file was saved or shared, device model, Android version, app version, country (derived from the IP address) and a random app-instance identifier.

  • Usage statistics never include your photos, signatures, file names or anything you type.
  • The app does not give Analytics your advertising ID or Android ID, and the data is not used for advertising.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, usage statistics are collected only after you agree on the consent form the app shows at start-up (storing information on your device and measuring content performance). You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. Everywhere else they are on by default.
  • You can turn usage statistics off in Settings → Share usage statistics. "Delete all data" in Settings also starts a new random identifier.

Firebase privacy information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

6. Data we receive

We (Tinypeak Studio) do not receive your photos, signatures, name, contact details or account information. We only see the anonymous totals from usage statistics and crash reports described above. The app has no accounts. If you email us for support, we receive what you choose to send and use it only to answer you.

7. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your data. Data collected by AdMob, Crashlytics and Analytics is processed by Google as described above.

8. Data retention and deletion

Photos and signatures are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage, for at most 24 hours (see section 1); copies prepared for saving or sharing are deleted on the same schedule. You can delete them at any time in Settings → Your data (the photo or signature in progress, saved custom sizes, or all data). Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage also deletes them immediately. Files you saved to your gallery or shared stay where you put them. Ad, crash and usage-statistics data are retained by Google according to its policies; Analytics data is kept for 2 months.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Not affiliated with any government

ID Photo Maker is not affiliated with any government agency. Requirements change: always check the current rules with the office that will receive your photo.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
